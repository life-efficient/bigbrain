# Article Reading Copy Cleanup

Use this rule for the indexed Markdown reading copy of an accepted article, including a Substack post or a substantive partial preview. Make the decision from the verified canonical body, after retrieval and the relevance gate. The goal is to retain the author's substantive account in its original wording and order while removing bounded calls to action that add no durable knowledge.

## Remove from the indexed reading copy

- Standalone requests to subscribe, upgrade to paid, share the newsletter, click a referral link, follow an account, or join a WhatsApp, Telegram, Discord, or similar channel.
- Standalone sponsorship, advertising, or partnership solicitations and their contact links or addresses.
- Repeated newsletter sign-off boilerplate whose only purpose is promotion, including blocks such as “Enjoying this newsletter? You can support my work by becoming a paid subscriber.”
- A promotional sentence embedded at the edge of a substantive paragraph only when it can be removed without changing the paragraph's claim or meaning. Otherwise keep the paragraph and, if useful, explain the limitation in the omission note.

Treat adjacent lines as one block when they form one call to action. Omit its link and contact address as part of that block. Do not leave a dead heading such as “Enjoying this newsletter?” in the indexed page.

## Keep

- Analysis, reported facts, evidence, examples, caveats, attributed quotations, and article-relevant links, even when they occur near a promotional block.
- Disclosures that affect interpretation, such as sponsorship of the article, conflicts of interest, affiliate relationships, or a paid product being assessed. Preserve the disclosure's substance without retaining an unrelated sign-up pitch.
- A promotion or announcement that is itself the article's subject, or a promotional post with substantive, source-specific information. Apply the normal source guidance and relevance gate to the whole post. Do not reject or hollow out a post merely because it is promotional.
- A paragraph when the promotional and substantive portions cannot be separated confidently. Mark the ambiguity rather than deleting potentially useful content.

## Preserve provenance and verify

1. Keep the fetched publisher bytes unchanged as a separate optional `.raw/` artifact when available and permitted. Never modify those bytes to make the reading copy appear original. If no raw artifact is retained, keep the canonical URL and retrieval details.
2. Record that the indexed copy is cleaned and identify each omitted block by type and source location, using a heading, paragraph position, or nearby opening words. Do not reproduce the removed sales copy or contact address in the note. If nothing was removed, no cleanup note is needed.
3. For a partial preview, retain only material actually visible before the access boundary. Apply the same cleanup to its indexed reading copy, and keep the preview-only label and boundary. A promotional block alone never qualifies as a substantive preview.
4. On same-Brain read-back, compare the indexed page with the accessible source or preserved raw artifact. Confirm that the remaining substantive text is verbatim and in order, every removal is logged, links needed for the article's argument remain, and no text from beyond a partial-preview boundary has appeared.
