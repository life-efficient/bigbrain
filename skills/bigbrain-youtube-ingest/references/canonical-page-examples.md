# Canonical Video Page Examples

Use these examples to shape the visible canonical Video page. They illustrate fidelity and reading form, not facts to copy into a real record.

## 1. Single-speaker explainer

### Raw source page

```text
[00:00:12] Today I want to explain why power, rather than land, is becoming the limit on data-centre expansion.
[00:00:19] A developer can acquire land, but cannot make an interconnection queue disappear.
[00:00:28] [Slide: regional map showing grid queues]
```

### Canonical Video page

```markdown
## Power is becoming the constraint

Power, rather than land, is becoming the limit on data-centre expansion. A developer can acquire land, but it cannot make an interconnection queue disappear.

![Regional map of grid-interconnection queues](source-captured-image-url)

The speaker uses the regional queue map to show why sites with secured power can be more valuable than sites with abundant land.
```

The canonical page retains the claim, its order, and the speaker's framing. It removes timestamp syntax and caption noise. Embed a visual only when it was actually shown or supplied by the source and is meaningful in context.

## 2. Interview or podcast

### Raw source page

```text
[00:14:02] Host: You say the constraint is not capital. What is it?
[00:14:07] Guest: It is the ability to convert power into usable capacity on a reliable timeline.
[00:14:15] Host: So the financing follows the interconnection?
[00:14:18] Guest: In the strongest projects, yes.
```

### Canonical Video page

```markdown
## What constrains expansion

**Host:** You say the constraint is not capital. What is it?

**Guest:** It is the ability to convert power into usable capacity on a reliable timeline.

**Host:** So the financing follows the interconnection?

**Guest:** In the strongest projects, yes.
```

Combine only short consecutive turns by the same speaker where doing so improves reading. Do not turn a conversation into a third-person summary or remove meaningful questions, disagreement, uncertainty, or qualification.

## Canonical page boundaries

- Use the schema summary for navigation only.
- Keep full timestamps, caption method, coverage notes, and exact raw wording on the separate raw source page.
- Do not add a thesis, Fact Sheet, executive summary, or claims that the source did not make.
- Preserve publication provenance and link from the canonical page to the raw source page.
